About The 1982 Guitar Company

The 1982 Guitar Company is run by Sam, a trained luthier, and music producer.

From the Studio to the Bench

Before lutherie, Sam spent years behind the glass as a music producer and recording engineer. Over his career in the studio, he worked on records for acclaimed artists including The Cribs, The Cars, R.E.M., U2, Snow Patrol, Bloc Party, and Weezer, and earned a Grammy nomination for his work with Taylor Swift.

Having spent decades working closely with artists, Sam understands exactly how a guitar needs to feel, respond, and inspire when it's in a player’s hands.

Traditional Luthier Training

After honing his woodworking skills in furniture-making classes in Los Angeles, a visit to friends in Athens, Georgia, sparked a turning point. There, Sam trained directly under master luthier Scott Baxendale in his renowned vintage guitar restoration program.

Under Scott’s mentorship, Sam specialized in the intricate art of rebuilding vintage Harmony, Kay, and Silvertone guitars. This process involves executing full structural neck resets, complete re-frets, and converting primitive ladder bracing to a custom X-Bracing system. The result is a guitar that plays with the precision of a brand-new instrument, but possesses the unmatched resonance of seasoned, 50-to-60-year-old tonewood.

Journey Back to Portsmouth

Following his training, Sam relocated to Seattle, running a dedicated guitar repair shop for three years before returning to the UK. Driven by a lifelong curiosity about human creativity and performance, he spent the post-pandemic era studying psychology. Today, alongside his luthiery work, he completes his MSc in Coaching Psychology and runs a private practice helping individuals in the creative and tech industries thrive.

The 1982 Guitar Company was born out of a desire to keep working with his hands, and building a new shop from the ground up. To satisfy that same passion for tactile, precise craftsmanship, Sam also operates Taskfix, a localized furniture assembly and precision handyman service across Portsmouth and Southsea.

Work With Us

The 1982 Guitar Company is going to grow from a compact bench space within an artist’s studio. The focus will be on precision setups, player-focused optimization, and essential maintenance at this time until the shop is more established and can accept greater volume of guitars and tools.

Whether your favorite gigging guitar needs its action perfectly dialed in, seasonal electronics troubleshooting, or you have a unique instrument you would like us to handle for you via consignment sales, drop us a line.

Because we operate strictly by appointment out of our Portsmouth studio, your instrument will receive our undivided, focused attention.
